The atelier

Made the slow way, on purpose.

Wendy began around one cutting table in a small Lisbon studio, with the idea that fewer, better garments could be a quiet form of resistance. Every piece is cut and finished by a team of four, in runs of 60 or fewer, from natural fibres sourced within Europe.

We alter anything you buy, for as long as you own it. If it can't be repaired, we'll take it back.
